By 2025, traditional testing methods—whether manual or heavily code-based—will be largely outdated. Enter no-code testing platforms, a transformative solution redefining how QA operates. Tools like Zof AI are already proving that no-code testing is not just the future of QA, but a necessity in modern development environments.
