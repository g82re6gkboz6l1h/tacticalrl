## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms are intuitive tools enabling software testing without coding expertise. Leveraging graphical interfaces, drag-and-drop options, and prebuilt workflows, they empower QA teams—even those without technical backgrounds—to create robust test cases. This innovation bridges the gap between technical and non-technical collaborators.
