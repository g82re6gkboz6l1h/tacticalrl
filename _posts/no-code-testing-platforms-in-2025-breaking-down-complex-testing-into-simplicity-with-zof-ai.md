## The Challenges Faced by Traditional Testing

Decades of conventional QA methods have highlighted persistent challenges, particularly in today’s fast-evolving software landscape:

#### 1. **Time-Consuming Processes**  
Labor-intensive manual scripting and prolonged debugging slow testing timelines in today’s fast-paced development cycles.

#### 2. **Higher Costs**  
Traditional testing teams often require highly specialized, expensive skillsets, increasing resource consumption and budgets.

#### 3. **Prone to Human Errors**  
Manual testing can lead to inconsistencies, while maintaining automated scripts demands ongoing expertise.

#### 4. **Limited Scalability**  
Legacy methods struggle to evolve with complex, multi-platform applications or large-scale projects.

These shortcomings demanded innovation. Enter no-code testing platforms, which promise to revolutionize QA for organizations of every scale.
