## Why Traditional QA Suffers From Bottlenecks

Despite the critical role QA plays, traditional testing practices come with various inefficiencies that slow down software delivery. Here’s a breakdown:

### 1. **Manual Testing Delays**
Manual tests consume countless man-hours, are vulnerable to errors, and often struggle to match iterative agile workflows. The constant rework and debugging can disrupt tightly planned timelines.

### 2. **Shortage of Skilled QA Engineers**
Automated testing frameworks require coding expertise. Unfortunately, the availability of such skills hasn’t kept pace with demand, leading to delays and capacity constraints.

### 3. **Developer-Dependent Testing Structures**
QA teams often depend on dev teams to resolve code dependencies, debug scripts, or stabilize the environment—a major blocker to streamlined workflows.

### 4. **Environment Setup Challenges**
Configuring tools and test environments can take considerable time, leading to further delays and inconsistent test results.

These challenges make traditional QA unsuitable for today’s rapid development cycles. **No-code platforms** like **Zof AI**, equipped with robust automation and user-friendly interfaces, are addressing these pain points head-on.
