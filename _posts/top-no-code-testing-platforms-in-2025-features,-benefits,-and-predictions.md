## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms empower users to create, execute, and oversee testing without writing code. These tools bridge developers, QA teams, and product managers by offering intuitive interfaces like drag-and-drop functionality and visual scripting. They are surging in popularity due to increasing software complexity and the need for faster testing cycles.
