## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing tools enable users to create, execute, and manage tests with drag-and-drop interfaces, visual workflows, and AI-driven optimizations—all without writing code. Unlike traditional automation solutions that rely on frameworks like Selenium, no-code platforms are accessible to non-technical personnel, fostering faster collaboration across teams.

### Core Advantages:
1. Simplify testing processes and eliminate coding bottlenecks.
2. Accelerate product release cycles while maintaining quality.
3. Democratize QA and empower product owners, designers, and analysts.

No-code solutions address critical issues in QA, such as reducing dependencies on coding expertise. With less time spent developing test scripts, businesses improve testing scalability and efficiency in response to evolving market demands.
