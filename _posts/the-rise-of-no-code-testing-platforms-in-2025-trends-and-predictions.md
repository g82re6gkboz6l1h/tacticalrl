In 2025, no-code testing platforms are taking center stage as software development emphasizes agility, speed, and collaboration. These tools allow teams to design, execute, and analyze tests without coding expertise, thanks to visual interfaces and AI-driven capabilities. Businesses are embracing no-code solutions to boost efficiency, lower costs, and adapt quickly to evolving software requirements.
