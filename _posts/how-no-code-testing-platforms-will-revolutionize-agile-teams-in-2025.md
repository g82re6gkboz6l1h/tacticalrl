## Why Agile Teams Need No-Code Testing Platforms

Modern Agile methodologies have redefined QA processes, emphasizing collaboration, flexibility, and rapid iteration. Yet, Agile teams face unique challenges:

- **Short Sprint Timelines:** With cycles lasting 1-2 weeks, there’s little room for lengthy QA phases.
- **Complex Architectures:** Applications are increasingly built with microservices, APIs, and regular updates.
- **Skill Gap:** Not all team members are equipped to write or execute advanced test scripts.

This is where no-code testing tools have stepped in, fundamentally transforming how Agile teams tackle software quality assurance.
