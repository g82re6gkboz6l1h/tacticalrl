## Why No-Code Testing Platforms Are the Future of QA

### Simplifying QA for Non-Developers
No-code platforms democratize software testing, allowing non-technical users such as product managers and marketers to create, execute, and manage test cases without writing a single line of code. This paradigm shift enables faster development cycles and more collaborative workflows.

### Overcoming Challenges of Traditional Testing Methods
The limitations of script-based testing—including dependency on technical expertise, high costs, slow feedback loops, and limited collaboration—make traditional methods less viable. No-code testing tools address these issues by being scalable, cost-effective, and user-friendly.
