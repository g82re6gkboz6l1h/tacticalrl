## What are No-Code Testing Platforms?
No-code testing platforms eliminate the need for writing complex scripts by offering drag-and-drop interfaces and AI-enhanced automation. This revolutionary approach simplifies the software testing process, allowing both technical and non-technical teams to execute comprehensive tests with minimal effort. By enabling rapid execution, these platforms drive faster product releases, enhance cross-platform compatibility, and reduce operational costs for businesses of all sizes.
