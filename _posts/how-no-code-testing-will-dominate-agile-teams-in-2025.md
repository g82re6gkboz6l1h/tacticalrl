## Challenges Agile Teams Face in Testing

Agile methodologies excel in fostering collaboration and fast iterations, but they also highlight the weaknesses of traditional testing approaches. Key challenges include:

- **Short sprints and release cycles:** Testing must align with rapid development timelines without delays.
- **Application complexity:** Modern software features APIs, microservices, and integrations that complicate testing.
- **Understaffed QA teams:** Limited resources restrict thorough testing and leave gaps in quality assurance.
- **Specialized skills gaps:** Coding-based testing creates knowledge silos that hinder team collaboration.

These issues demand innovative solutions—and no-code testing platforms are uniquely positioned to fill the gaps.
