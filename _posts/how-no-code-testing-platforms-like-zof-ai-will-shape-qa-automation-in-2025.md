## Understanding QA Automation Challenges

Automated testing is the backbone of software development, but conventional methods come with significant hurdles:

### **1. High Complexity**
QA teams often contend with intricate systems that require time-intensive scripting.

### **2. Dependency on Coding Expertise**
Traditional frameworks rely on programming skills, creating bottlenecks for non-coding teams.

### **3. Resource Drain**
Legacy systems demand substantial time and cost investments.

### **4. Scaling Limitations**
Growing software ecosystems challenge the adaptability of traditional tools.
