## What is No-Code Testing?

No-code testing eliminates the need for manual coding by leveraging drag-and-drop interfaces and visual workflows. This shift enhances productivity and expands access to users without technical expertise. By 2025, no-code testing will address common development bottlenecks, foster team collaboration, and integrate seamlessly into CI/CD environments. As rapid development cycles demand efficiency, no-code solutions, powered by platforms like [Zof AI](https://zof.ai), will reign supreme.
