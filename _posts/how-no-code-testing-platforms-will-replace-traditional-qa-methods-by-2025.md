## The Drawbacks of Traditional QA Methods in Modern Development

Traditional QA processes have long supported the industry, yet they face critical limitations:

1. **Time-Intensive Setup**: Writing complex test scripts demands programming expertise, slowing development.
2. **High Costs**: Skilled teams are resource-heavy amid rising salaries.
3. **Rigid Processes**: Constant code changes are tough to address without frequent delays.
4. **Limited Collaboration**: Teams often work in siloes, creating inefficiencies.

These challenges have set the stage for no-code testing platforms to disrupt the industry.

3. **Scalability**: Cloud-based no-code solutions efficiently handle testing across devices, platforms, and browsers.
