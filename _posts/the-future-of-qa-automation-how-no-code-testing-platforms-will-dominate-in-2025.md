Why Organizations Choose No-Code Platforms

1. **Ease of Use:** Simplified interfaces minimize the need for coding expertise.
2. **Accelerated Time-to-Market:** Faster test creation and automation streamline product launches.
3. **Cost Efficiency:** Cuts hiring costs and reduces the expense of technical test tools.
4. **Scalable Testing:** AI-enabled platforms make scaling tests seamlessly manageable.
5. **AI Integration:** Advanced functionalities optimize anomalies detection and automate routine test creation.
