## Challenges of No-Code Testing Platforms in 2025 

While no-code testing platforms are user-friendly, their wider adoption comes with specific challenges:

### 1. **Limited Customization**
Pre-built templates may restrict flexibility for unique testing requirements, hindering their usability in complex software projects.

### 2. **Scalability Issues**
Many no-code platforms struggle to manage large-scale, intricate testing scenarios without sacrificing performance.

### 3. **Knowledge Gaps**
Non-technical users often face difficulties understanding test implications, impacting platform effectiveness unless proper training is provided.

### 4. **Integration Roadblocks**
Seamless integration into existing CI/CD pipelines, project management tools, and developer workflows often remains challenging.

### 5. **Surface-Level Testing**
No-code tools frequently focus on basic UI testing, leaving critical areas like API, backend, or security testing under-addressed.
