## Challenges QA Teams Face Today

Modern QA teams grapple with a variety of obstacles:

### 1. Increased Complexity
With multi-platform applications, APIs, and interconnected services, QA teams now handle intricate systems that demand rigorous testing for performance, security, and scalability.

### 2. Shorter Time-to-Market Cycles
Agile and DevOps environments increase pressure on QA teams to speed up testing as businesses demand faster delivery.

### 3. Growing Skill Gaps
QA teams often struggle to find professionals with advanced programming knowledge, especially for traditional script-based testing.

### 4. Budget Constraints
Building and running traditional testing frameworks can be time-consuming and expensive, leaving many QA teams constrained by resources.
