## Why No-Code QA Tools Were Needed

Traditional QA automation posed critical challenges that limited efficiency, scalability, and collaboration:

1. **Requiring Advanced Technical Skills**: Tools like Selenium and Appium demanded coding expertise, excluding non-technical teams.
2. **Slower Test Creation and Updates**: Manual scripting prolonged processes, complicating iteration.
3. **High-Cost Maintenance**: Conventional frameworks were expensive as test cases needed frequent revisions.
4. **Poor Scalability**: QA often failed to match fast-paced development in Agile and DevOps environments.
5. **Limited Collaboration**: Non-technical stakeholders were excluded, leaving gaps in communication.

These barriers hinted at the need for innovation—and no-code tools delivered that.
