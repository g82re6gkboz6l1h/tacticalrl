### 2. **Wide Test Coverage**
From functional testing to API-level and cross-browser testing, platforms should support comprehensive testing without limitations.

### 5. **Seamless Integrations**
The platform should integrate smoothly with CI/CD pipelines and tools like Jira, Slack, and version control systems for a unified workflow.
