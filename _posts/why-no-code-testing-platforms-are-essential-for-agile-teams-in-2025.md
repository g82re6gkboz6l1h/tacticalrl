No-code testing platforms simplify and democratize quality assurance processes by eliminating the need for coding. They empower teams to test quickly and efficiently, meeting the needs of fast-paced Agile environments.

2. **Agile-Friendly Integration:** No-code tools fit seamlessly into CI/CD pipelines.
