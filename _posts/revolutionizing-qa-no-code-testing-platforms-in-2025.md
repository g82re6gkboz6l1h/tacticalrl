No-code testing platforms enable teams to design, execute, and analyze tests without coding expertise. With AI-driven automation, drag-and-drop interfaces, and pre-designed templates, testing is now faster, cheaper, and accessible to non-developers. As we approach 2025, these platforms, including **Zof AI**, are becoming the preferred standard.
