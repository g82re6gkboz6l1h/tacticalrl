The Business Case: Cost and Time Savings with No-Code Solutions

Traditional QA methods involve hiring coding specialists, creating lengthy scripts, and maintaining them as systems evolve. No-code platforms reduce expenses by saving development time and minimizing hiring costs. For example, leading platforms like [Zof AI](https://zof.ai) streamline processes such as test case maintenance and enable shorter onboarding periods.

Startups benefit from accelerated release cycles, and enterprises value easy integration with existing workflows for scaling projects seamlessly.
