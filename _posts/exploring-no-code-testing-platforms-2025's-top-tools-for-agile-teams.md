## Why Use No-Code Testing Tools in Agile Processes?

No-code platforms address two key challenges agile teams face: fast iteration cycles and simplified collaboration. Traditional testing relied heavily on developers, but no-code platforms empower non-technical team members such as QA testers and product managers to achieve the following:

- Speed up test creation and execution.
- Collaborate across teams with simplified processes.
- Accelerate sprints by eliminating bottlenecks.
- Reduce operational costs by lowering reliance on developers for testing.
