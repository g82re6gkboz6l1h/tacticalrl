## Key Innovations in QA Tools Over the Last Decade

A decade ago, QA primarily relied on code-heavy frameworks like Selenium and Appium, designed for technically skilled professionals. Creating and maintaining test suites demanded knowledge of programming and familiarity with complex infrastructure, often resulting in siloed processes and delays in product rollouts.

Between 2015 and 2023, the introduction of no-code and low-code platforms, including cutting-edge innovations from companies like [Zof AI](https://zof.ai), transformed QA workflows. These platforms simplified test creation with user-friendly interfaces and automatic test generation, empowering developers, professionals, and even non-technical stakeholders to actively contribute to the QA lifecycle. Today in 2025, the no-code revolution has brought QA to the masses, driving faster software delivery and higher-quality products.
