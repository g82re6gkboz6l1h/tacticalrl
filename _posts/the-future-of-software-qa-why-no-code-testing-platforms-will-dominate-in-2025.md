## Introduction to No-Code Testing Platforms

Software testing has traditionally depended on script-based automation and manual processes, demanding technical expertise and consuming significant time. Features like drag-and-drop functionality, AI integrations, and pre-built templates empower both technical and non-technical stakeholders to streamline QA workflows and maximize productivity.

### 3. **Lower Costs**
Reduce overhead by allowing non-developers to contribute to test creation.

### 4. **Improved Collaboration**
Enable teams to work together seamlessly, bridging gaps between technical and non-technical members.

### 5. **Adaptability for Agile and DevOps**
Integrating seamlessly into CI/CD pipelines makes continuous testing easier.
