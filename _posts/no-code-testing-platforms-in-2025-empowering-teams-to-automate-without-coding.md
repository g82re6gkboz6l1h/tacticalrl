## Overcoming Challenges in Traditional Testing

Traditional testing often faces hurdles such as:

### High Resource Demands
Automation frameworks require skilled developers and ongoing maintenance, adding time and expense.

### Limited Scalability
Complex workflows and growing test cases hinder scaling capabilities, creating delays in agile pipelines.

### Slow Feedback Mechanisms
Manual testing slows iterative development cycles, delaying releases.

### Team Disconnects
Communication gaps between testers and developers can lead to missed bugs or flawed logic.

These issues obstruct teams from achieving agility and innovation. Fortunately, no-code testing platforms address these barriers, transforming workflows.
