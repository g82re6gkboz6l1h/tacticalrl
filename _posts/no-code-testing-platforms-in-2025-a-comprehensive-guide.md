## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms allow users to design, execute, and manage software tests without any programming knowledge. Featuring user-friendly drag-and-drop interfaces, these tools empower non-tech-savvy individuals, including business analysts and product managers, to take part in the testing lifecycle. 

### Why Choose No-Code Testing?
These platforms democratize the testing process, allowing businesses to:

- **Cut development costs** by reducing dependency on specialized developers.
- **Save time** with rapid test creation and execution workflows.
- **Achieve scalability** at an enterprise level effortlessly.
- **Streamline adaptability** to changing requirements and customer demands.

By eliminating the need for manual coding, no-code platforms enable teams to hyper-focus on delivering high-quality products promptly.
