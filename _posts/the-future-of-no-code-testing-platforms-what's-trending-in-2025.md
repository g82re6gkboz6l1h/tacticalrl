## Introduction to No-Code Testing Platforms

No-code testing platforms allow users to design, implement, and execute software tests without writing a single line of code. These platforms leverage drag-and-drop interfaces, templates, and intuitive workflows designed for seamless adoption by non-technical users. Initially created to improve efficiency for agile teams, no-code solutions have grown to address a wider range of challenges in software testing.
