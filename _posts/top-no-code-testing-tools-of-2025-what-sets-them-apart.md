## Why No-Code Testing Tools Matter

No-code platforms simplify the testing process through drag-and-drop interfaces, eliminating coding needs and making quality assurance accessible across teams. Key advantages include:

- **Faster Testing Cycles:** Save time with intuitive test case creation.
- **Reduced Costs:** Minimize the dependence on specialized QA roles.
- **Collaboration:** Engage both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- **Scalability:** Meet the needs of complex workflows with cloud-based tools.
