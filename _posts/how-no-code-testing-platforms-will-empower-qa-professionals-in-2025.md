## Major Challenges QA Teams Face

### Complexity in Software Testing
The complexity of software applications often requires QA teams to simulate multi-layered user interactions across diverse platforms—an intricate and time-consuming endeavor.

### Speed vs. Thoroughness in Testing
The era of Agile and DevOps demands faster testing cycles without compromising quality. Often, speed comes at the cost of in-depth testing.

### Dependency on Technical Skills
Traditional testing requires proficiency in programming languages like Python and Java, which limits opportunities for non-technical professionals.

### Resource Limitations
Shrinking budgets and tight deadlines force teams to work with fewer resources, leading to potential inefficiencies and burnout.
