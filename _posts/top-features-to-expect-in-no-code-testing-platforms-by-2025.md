No-code testing platforms began by simplifying repetitive processes with user-friendly interfaces and workflows. ### AI Revolution in No-Code Testing
The integration of **AI-driven technologies** has supercharged these platforms, automating complex testing processes and maximizing efficiency with minimal human intervention. As AI continues to evolve, no-code testing platforms will further streamline quality assurance, making them indispensable by 2025.
