## QA’s Core Challenges: Why Scaling is Difficult

Scaling QA operations poses unique challenges for growing organizations. Here are five common hurdles:

### 1. **High Costs of QA Expansion**
Traditional tools demand a significant investment in skilled personnel, robust infrastructure, and specialized software.

### 2. **Time-Consuming Manual Testing**
Repetitive processes like regression testing are prone to human error and can drag release cycles.

### 3. **Skill Gaps in QA Teams**
Finding and training specialists in traditional frameworks remains challenging and costly.

### 4. **Scalability Constraints**
Traditional tools often fail to adapt to rapidly growing, interconnected systems.

### 5. **Agile and DevOps Compatibility Issues**
In Agile and DevOps settings, traditional QA can become a bottleneck. Seamlessly embedding QA into fast-paced workflows remains a struggle.
