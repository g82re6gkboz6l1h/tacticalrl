## History and Evolution of No-Code Testing Platforms

The journey of testing tools mirrors technological advancement in software development. From manual testing to code-heavy platforms like Selenium, QA methodologies have evolved with frameworks like Agile and DevOps. However, scripting-heavy solutions proved cumbersome for non-tech-savvy testers. With the emergence of **no-code technologies**, QA moved into a new era—equipped with drag-and-drop interfaces and AI-powered insights.

By 2025, these platforms are democratizing testing by bridging expertise gaps, empowering diverse teams, and delivering faster product innovations.
