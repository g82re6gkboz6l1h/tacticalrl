## The Evolution of Testing: A Journey to No-Code

Testing methodologies have evolved in three phases:

1. **Manual Testing**: Relied on human involvement, prone to inefficiencies and errors at scale.
2. **Automation Testing**: Introduced tools like Selenium and Appium but demanded advanced technical expertise and maintenance.
3. **No-Code Testing**: Empowers users via visual workflows, AI algorithms, and scalable solutions. Platforms like **Zof AI** meet the demands of modern Agile and DevOps methodologies by fostering collaboration and inclusive QA processes.
