## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms allow users to create, manage, and execute test cases through visual workflows—eliminating the need for extensive programming knowledge. These tools utilize drag-and-drop functionality, AI-driven automation, and intuitive interfaces to simplify testing. Traditionally reserved for engineers, testing is now accessible to product managers, QA specialists, and even non-technical contributors.

### Key Benefits:
- **Faster Testing:** Automated workflows reduce repetitive tasks.
- **Increased Accessibility:** Non-developers can easily create test cases.
- **Cost Efficiency:** Minimizes reliance on engineers, saving time and resources.

By 2025, no-code testing platforms are expected to further evolve, fostering greater participation across teams and driving innovation in testing workflows.
