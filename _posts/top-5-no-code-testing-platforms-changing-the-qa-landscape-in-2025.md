## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms are software solutions designed to automate and simplify the testing process through visually intuitive workflows, drag-and-drop interfaces, and AI-driven automation. They enable team members from diverse backgrounds, such as product managers and business analysts, to participate in QA efforts. By reducing reliance on technical specialists, these platforms streamline testing pipelines and boost collaboration.

Key features include:

- **End-to-End Automation:** Comprehensive testing workflows, from UI to functionality.
- **User-Friendly Interfaces:** Easy-to-navigate tools requiring no prior coding skills.
- **CI/CD Pipeline Integration:** Compatibility with modern development tools like Jenkins and GitLab.
