## The Journey of QA: From Manual Testing to No-Code Platforms

**QA practices have come a long way.** Decades ago, manual testing was the norm—labor-intensive, slow, and heavily reliant on technical expertise. Then came automation pioneers like Selenium, JUnit, and Appium, allowing test suites to be programmed and reused. However, these required strong coding skills, making them daunting for small or under-resourced teams.

### No-Code Solutions: Ushering in Change
No-code testing platforms democratized testing by enabling anyone—regardless of technical expertise—to craft, execute, and scale tests. Features like drag-and-drop workflows, intuitive presets, and pre-built templates define these tools, removing coding bottlenecks. As agile and DevOps models dominate the software sphere in 2025, no-code platforms like Zof AI have transitioned from being luxury tools to absolute necessities.
