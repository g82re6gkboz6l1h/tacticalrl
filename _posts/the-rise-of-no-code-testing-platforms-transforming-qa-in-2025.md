## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code platforms enable users to create, run, and maintain tests without coding knowledge. These platforms use intuitive UIs, drag-and-drop tools, and visual workflows, making QA accessible to non-technical team members such as project managers. By simplifying testing processes, these platforms empower businesses to focus on delivering high-quality software faster.
