No-code testing tools empower QA professionals to design and execute tests without coding. With drag-and-drop visual interfaces, prebuilt templates, and AI-driven test case creation, these platforms open up testing to non-technical team members, democratizing the process. Tools like [Zof AI](https://zof.ai) are pioneering the no-code landscape, offering advanced features that simplify even the most complex testing needs.
