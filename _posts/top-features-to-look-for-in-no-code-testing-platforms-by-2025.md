## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms eliminate the need for manual coding in test creation. These platforms rely on intuitive interfaces like **drag-and-drop functionality**, **pre-configured modules**, and **visual workflows**, enabling QA testers, business analysts, and product managers to contribute seamlessly to the testing process.

As agile methodologies and DevOps dominate the software development space, no-code platforms solve critical challenges: reducing time-to-market, improving collaboration, and addressing skillset gaps.
