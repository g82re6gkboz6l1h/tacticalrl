## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms tackle the core challenges of traditional QA—complexity, time investment, and technical expertise demands. Unlike conventional methods that require coding proficiency, these platforms offer intuitive drag-and-drop interfaces and pre-configured workflows, empowering even non-technical teams to contribute to QA.

### Why Is No-Code Testing a Game-Changer?
- Breaks down technical barriers for non-specialist users
- Enables faster development cycles
- Aligns seamlessly with agile and continuous delivery goals

By 2025, no-code tools are driving supercharged QA automation, bolstered by innovative AI integrations.
