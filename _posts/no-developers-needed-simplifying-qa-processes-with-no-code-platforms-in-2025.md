## How QA Typically Relied on Developers in the Past

### Key Challenges in Traditional QA

- **Developer Dependency:** Writing and maintaining test scripts required specialized knowledge in programming languages like Python or JavaScript.
- **High Costs:** Recruitment of skilled QA developers often created financial strain—especially for startups.
- **Limited Agility:** Non-technical team members were excluded, resulting in slower and less collaborative workflows.
