## Challenges to Scaling QA Teams

### 1. **Resource Limitations**
Manual and standard automated testing require skilled professionals, making scalability a financial and operational concern.

### 2. **Complex Test Scenarios**
As applications grow interconnected, QA faces difficulties simulating realistic scenarios efficiently.

### 3. **Fragmented Team Collaboration**
Miscommunication among QA, development, and operations leads to inefficiencies and testing gaps.

### 4. **Compromised Test Coverage**
Time constraints force teams to choose between comprehensive coverage and project deadlines.
