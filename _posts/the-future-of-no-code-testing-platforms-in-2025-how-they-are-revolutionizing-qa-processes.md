## What are No-Code Testing Platforms? A Quick Overview

No-code testing platforms simplify software testing by eliminating coding requirements. With intuitive drag-and-drop interfaces, automated testing capabilities, and AI integration, these platforms make testing accessible to everyone—from seasoned QA engineers to non-technical business leaders.

### Challenges Addressed by No-Code Tools:
- **Skill Gaps**: Opens QA to non-programmers.
- **Speed Boost**: Reduces time for test creation and execution compared to manual scripting.
- **Cost Reduction**: Lowers dependency on expensive consultants and large QA teams.
