## The QA Evolution: From Manual to No-Code Testing

Decades ago, QA relied on manual testing—time-consuming, prone to human error, and unsustainable in fast-moving software development cycles. Automation improved on these challenges, introducing scripted tests. However, coding expertise became a bottleneck.

**No-code testing platforms** now represent the next leap. With drag-and-drop interfaces, visual workflows, and AI features, testers with minimal programming knowledge can design and maintain test cases. This democratization accelerates project lifecycles while involving cross-functional teams.
