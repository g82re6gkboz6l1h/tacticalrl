## What are No-Code Testing Platforms?
No-code testing platforms allow users to create and execute tests with intuitive drag-and-drop interfaces and AI-powered tools, removing the need for coding expertise. This opens QA processes to broader teams, from marketing to product managers, fostering collaboration while reducing reliance on engineers.
