## Overview: What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms utilize user-friendly interfaces, such as drag-and-drop tools, to design, execute, and analyze tests without the need for programming skills. Unlike traditional testing that demands specialized expertise, no-code testing enables Agile teams to build, test, and iterate faster while ensuring high-quality outputs.

These platforms eliminate bottlenecks in QA workflows, reduce technical dependencies, and allow for real-time collaboration between developers, testers, and non-technical stakeholders—key to Agile success.
