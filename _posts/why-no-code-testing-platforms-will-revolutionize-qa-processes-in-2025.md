## The Drawbacks of Traditional QA Testing

Despite its vital role, conventional QA testing faces significant limitations:

### **1. Limited Accessibility for Non-Technicians**  
Most QA testing relies on coding skills, narrowing access and straining resources. Non-technical contributors often feel sidelined, creating inefficiencies.

### **2. Time-Consuming Processes**  
Writing, debugging, and maintaining test cases extend release cycles. Agile and DevOps teams are often delayed by these traditional testing methods.

### **3. Burdensome Maintenance Costs**  
Complex tools and heavy reliance on manual testing drive up expenses, making traditional QA testing a costly investment.

### **4. Collaboration Gaps**  
Traditional testing workflows often operate in silos, leading to weak communication between testers, developers, and business stakeholders.

These limitations highlight the need for a simpler, more effective approach — one that no-code platforms promise to deliver.
