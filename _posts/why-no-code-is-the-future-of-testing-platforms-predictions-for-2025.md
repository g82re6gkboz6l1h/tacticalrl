## Overview of Modern Testing Platforms

### Key Challenges in Current QA Practices:
1. **Complex Tech Environments:** Testing today's microservices, APIs, and cloud-based applications requires niche expertise.
2. **Automation Bottlenecks:** Heavy resource investment and coding dependencies persist in traditional automation tools like Selenium.
3. **Demand for Accelerated Delivery:** DevOps prioritizes continuous delivery, but QA often lags behind.
4. **Siloed Collaboration:** Testing is isolated from broader teams like product and marketing, limiting effectiveness.
