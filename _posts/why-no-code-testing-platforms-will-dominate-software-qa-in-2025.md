These platforms come equipped with visual interfaces, drag-and-drop functionality, and ready-made templates. They simplify QA for users across the board by eliminating the need for traditional programming expertise.

## How QA Workflows Have Evolved  
A look at QA's evolution reveals how we’ve arrived at the no-code era:  

### 1. Manual Testing Origins
Initially, QA relied solely on manual testing, which was thorough but slow, prone to errors, and notoriously difficult to scale.  

### 2. Automation Revolution
Frameworks like Selenium ushered in automation, streamlining repetitive tasks. However, it demanded programming expertise, heavily dependent on skilled engineers.  

### 3. Low-Code Testing Emergence
Low-code solutions bridged gaps between manual and automated approaches, offering partial ease of use. However, these tools still had coding elements, hindering accessibility.  

### 4. No-Code Transformation
No-code turns testing on its head—eliminating scripting entirely, enhancing efficiency, and democratizing workflows. Tools like Zof AI leverage advancements like AI-driven automation, offering scalable and easy-to-use testing solutions.
