## Why Manual Testing Is Fading in 2025

Manual testing once dominated software QA, requiring teams of testers to review applications meticulously. While effective, manual testing faces challenges in today’s fast-paced software development cycles:

1. **Complex Applications**: Modern apps rely on microservices, APIs, and cross-platform compatibility, which are tedious to test manually.

2. **Agile & DevOps Needs**: Development cycles today demand rapid testing, impossible to achieve with manual methods.

3. **Resource-Heavy**: Manual testing requires skilled workers, consuming significant resources and time.

4. **Human Error**: Complex scenarios are prone to oversight, resulting in less accurate testing.

As a result, companies are adopting no-code platforms that offer automation and efficiency, transforming QA practices for the better.
