1. **Ease of Use**: Look for an intuitive design, drag-and-drop interfaces, and guided workflows to reduce onboarding time.

4. **Scalability**: Whether handling small apps or large enterprise platforms, the tool should scale effortlessly.

Pros and Cons of No-Code Testing Platforms

### Benefits:
- **Accessibility**: Inclusivity for non-technical users.
- **Speed**: Faster testing cycles compared to traditional methods.
- **Cost Efficiency**: Reduced reliance on expert developers or QA engineers.
- **Automation**: Intelligent tools offer features like **self-healing test cases**.
- **Collaboration**: Simplify cross-functional teamwork.

### Challenges:
- **Limited Flexibility**: Customization may be restricted compared to script-based tools.
- **Scalability Issues**: Many tools struggle with large-scale enterprise-level app testing.
- **Tool Dependence**: Heavy reliance on vendor support for updates or compatibility.
