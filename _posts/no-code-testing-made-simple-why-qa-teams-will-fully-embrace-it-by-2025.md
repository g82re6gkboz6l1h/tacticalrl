## Challenges QA Teams Face with Traditional Testing Tools

### 1. **Complex Automation Frameworks**
Conventional tools demand deep coding expertise, forcing testers to navigate scripting languages such as Java, Python, or JavaScript—leading to inefficiencies and steep learning curves.

### 2. **Time-Intensive Test Development**
Weeks of effort are often required to create, debug, and maintain scripts, delaying project timelines.

### 3. **Limited Cross-Team Collaboration**
Siloed workflows arise when non-technical stakeholders, such as product managers and designers, struggle to interpret code-based testing frameworks.

### 4. **Cumbersome Maintenance**
Rapid application updates require continuous script revisions, consuming valuable QA time.

### 5. **Skill Dependence**
Traditional frameworks demand niche programming skills, resulting in higher costs and reduced agility.
