## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms streamline the testing lifecycle, empowering QA professionals to create, execute, and manage tests via intuitive drag-and-drop interfaces instead of traditional coding. The broader no-code ecosystem has made software development accessible, and these platforms are extending that accessibility to testing.

With AI-driven automation, pre-built templates, and smart workflows, these tools eliminate the reliance on developers for creating test cases. In 2025, the maturity and global acceptance of no-code technologies have reached unprecedented levels, largely driven by agile development and rapid product iteration strategies.
