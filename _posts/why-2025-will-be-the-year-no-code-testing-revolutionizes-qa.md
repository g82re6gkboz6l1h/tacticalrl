## Challenges Faced in Traditional Software Testing

In its current form, software testing often struggles to keep pace with fast-moving development cycles. Here are the pain points hindering traditional QA methodologies:

### **1. Resource-Intensive Process**
Technical expertise is indispensable for writing and managing test scripts, making testing expensive and prone to bottlenecks. Smaller companies often fall behind due to limited resources.

### **2. Scalability Issues**
As applications evolve, testing requirements grow exponentially. Traditional workflows requiring manual script creation for each update slow innovation and create scalability barriers.

### **3. Error-Prone Workflows**
Fatigue and repetitive manual tasks lead to errors, missed bugs, and compromised user experiences.

### **4. Delayed Time-to-Market**
Conventional methods prolong development cycles, negatively impacting market opportunities and customer satisfaction.

The limitations of traditional practices are fueling the adoption of no-code testing as a smarter, faster alternative.
