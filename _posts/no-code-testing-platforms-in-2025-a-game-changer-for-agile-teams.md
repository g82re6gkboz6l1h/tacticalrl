## The Role of No-Code Testing Platforms in Agile Teams

Agile processes thrive on flexibility and rapid iteration, but testing often struggles to keep pace due to complex infrastructures or reliance on highly technical skillsets. The advent of no-code testing solutions revolutionizes QA by enabling any team member to create and execute tests, regardless of coding expertise. By reducing friction between development and testing workflows, these platforms expedite delivery and improve collaboration.
