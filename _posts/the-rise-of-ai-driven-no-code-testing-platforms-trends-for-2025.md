By 2025, over 70% of new enterprise applications will rely on no-code or low-code systems. This adoption elevates the need for robust, AI-enabled automated testing tools.

### 2. Managing Complex Applications
Modern apps span web, mobile, and IoT—demanding seamless integration, security, and performance validation. AI-driven platforms excel by adapting to multisystem requirements.

### 3. AI Breakthroughs
Technological advancements, from deep learning to generative AI, are embedding smarter capabilities into testing platforms, ensuring continuous improvement and optimized processes.
