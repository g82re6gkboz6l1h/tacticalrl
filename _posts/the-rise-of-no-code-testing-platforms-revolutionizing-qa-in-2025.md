## Introduction to No-Code Testing Platforms

No-code testing platforms remove traditional barriers in QA by enabling tests via drag-and-drop interfaces and visual workflows. Non-technical professionals can conduct tests without coding knowledge, saving time and money. These platforms are democratizing QA processes, matching broader trends for accessible technology across different sectors.
