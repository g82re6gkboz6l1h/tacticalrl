## Challenges QA Teams Face Without No-Code Tools
QA teams relying on coding-heavy frameworks often experience these hurdles:

### 1. **Time-Intensive Processes**
Conventional tools require substantial coding for automated test cases, making QA workflows slow and resource-dependent. As fast iterations become essential, delays hinder efficiency and progress.

### 2. **Exclusion of Non-Technical Professionals**
Code-focused testing frameworks prevent product managers and UX teams from contributing crucial insights, leaving gaps in alignment with user expectations.

### 3. **Poor Scalability**
Large enterprises struggle to maintain massive test scripts across diverse integrations. Traditional tools often fail to scale with modern, global applications.

### 4. **Rising Costs**
Coding frameworks demand costly specialized talent, making scaling impractical for startups or enterprises with limited budgets.

### 5. **Lack of Agility**
CI/CD environments demand real-time adaptability, but traditional tools fall short of delivering the agility required for high-speed development cycles.
