## Traditional Testing: The Challenges QA Teams Face

Conventional QA methods require significant technical skills, time, and resources. Key pain points include:

- **Complexity**: Test automation tools demand programming expertise, creating barriers for many teams.
- **Budget Constraints**: High costs make robust testing inaccessible for startups.
- **Scalability Issues**: Growing applications slow down traditional testing.
- **Feedback Delays**: Lengthy processes hinder real-time responses to development needs.
