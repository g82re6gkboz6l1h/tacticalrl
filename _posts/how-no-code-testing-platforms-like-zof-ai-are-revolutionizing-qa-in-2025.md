## Fixing the Flaws in Traditional QA Approaches

Before the breakthrough of platforms like Zof AI, QA teams endured numerous inefficiencies and roadblocks:

### 1. **Dependence on Specialists**
Testing scripts required advanced coding, which limited participation to technical experts. Hiring, training, and retaining such talent posed significant costs.

### 2. **Prolonged Development Timelines**
Manual scripting and constant revisions of test cases slowed down workflows, hampering faster deliveries.

### 3. **Fragile Test Scripts**
Automated tests often broke due to small app updates, disrupting operations and requiring time-consuming fixes.

### 4. **Fragmented Collaboration**
Communication barriers between testers and stakeholders delayed issue resolution and inhibited collaborative insights.

### 5. **Limited Scalability**
Older QA systems were unsuited for expanding demands, especially in multi-platform or enterprise-grade deployments.

These challenges underscored the industry’s need for smarter, scalable solutions. Enter no-code platforms, which provided a much-needed shift in methodology.
