## Agile Testing Challenges in 2025

In 2025, the software industry faces unprecedented hurdles in agile testing practices:

1. **System Complexity**: Advancements in IoT, AI, and blockchain have compounded software intricacies.
2. **Skill Gaps**: The rising demand for tech-savvy QA teams often outweighs available resources.
3. **Shorter Delivery Cycles**: Agile sprints require faster, more efficient testing processes.
4. **Global Application Scalability**: Testing across diverse environments and user demographics increases complexity.
5. **Continuous Integration Challenges**: Seamless CI/CD testing pipelines are vital yet elusive.

No-code testing platforms are key to addressing these obstacles efficiently and accelerating agile workflows.
