## Key Challenges of Agile Without Advanced QA Tools

Agile thrives on its iterative and collaborative nature. However, these qualities can cause QA roadblocks without the right tools. Common challenges include:

- **Time-consuming manual testing**: Slows down releases as teams scramble to meet deadlines.
- **Lack of automation expertise**: Many teams have limited technical resources for complex test scripting.
- **Low integration with CI/CD**: Testing often disrupts seamless Continuous Delivery pipelines.
- **Inconsistent test coverage**: Tight schedules ensure some bugs go undetected.
- **Soaring QA costs**: Testing requires significant resources, increasing resource expenditure.
