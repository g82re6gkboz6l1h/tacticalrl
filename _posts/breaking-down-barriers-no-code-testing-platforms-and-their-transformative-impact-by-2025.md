## **Why Traditional QA Is No Longer Enough**

The challenges of traditional QA methods often derail development timelines. Here’s what the key issues look like:

- **Skill Gaps**: Requires knowledge of complex programming and testing tools, leaving non-technical staff sidelined.
- **Resource Load**: Code-dependent testing frameworks demand ongoing maintenance, increasing costs.
- **Slow Bug Resolution**: Delays result from time-consuming testing cycles and feedback loops.
- **Limited Scalability**: Traditional testing struggles with modern software complexities like IoT, mobile apps, and cloud computing.

No-code testing platforms eliminate these pain points, introducing game-changing capabilities that empower technical and non-technical team members to collaborate efficiently.
