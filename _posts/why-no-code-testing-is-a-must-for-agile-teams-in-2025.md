## Challenges of Traditional QA in Agile Development

Traditional QA processes cannot keep up with Agile’s fast-paced development cycles. Here’s why:

### 1. **Manual Testing Slows Progress**
Manual QA is labor-intensive and susceptible to human error, delaying sprints and reducing product quality.

### 2. **Rigid Frameworks**
Detailed test scripts take time to create. When Agile teams pivot their priorities, QA often lags behind.

### 3. **Demand for Specialized Skills**
Traditional automated testing needs programming expertise, which smaller teams might lack.

### 4. **Costly Maintenance**
Scripts need constant updates when functionality changes, quickly driving up costs and impacting cycle time.

These challenges highlight the need for modern, adaptable solutions like no-code testing.
