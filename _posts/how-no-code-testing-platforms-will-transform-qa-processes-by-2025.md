## What QA Will Look Like in 2025

Traditional testing is losing ground to more advanced methods. By 2025, QA teams will embrace:  
1. **Automation First** – Reduced reliance on manual testing eliminates errors and boosts productivity.  
2. **No-Code Revolution** – Democratizing QA for all team members with intuitive tools.  
3. **Shift-Left Testing** – Issue detection early in the development cycle minimizes costly errors.  
4. **AI Integration** – Intelligent tools predict issues and streamline workflows with precision.

This shift promises faster, effective, and scalable QA solutions.
