## What is No-Code Testing?

No-code testing allows QA teams and non-technical stakeholders to design, execute, and analyze automated tests without requiring programming skills. Through visual workflows and intuitive UX, even non-developers can actively contribute to QA efforts.

Historically, software testing was labor-intensive and script-driven, demanding high technical expertise. However, as software complexity surged, the demand for agile and efficient solutions grew. No-code testing platforms address this gap, empowering diverse teams to create automated tests faster and reducing dependency on traditional coding-heavy models.

By eliminating scripting challenges, speeding up delivery timelines, and fostering collaboration across departments, no-code testing platforms are transforming how QA is conducted in today’s fast-paced tech environment.
