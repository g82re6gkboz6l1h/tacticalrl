## Introduction to No-Code Testing Platforms
No-code testing platforms simplify QA by enabling teams to design, execute, and manage tests without any programming skills. Using intuitive visual interfaces, these tools empower QA engineers, developers, and non-technical collaborators to work seamlessly together. By 2025, industries like fintech, healthcare, and e-commerce will widely embrace no-code solutions, accelerating software delivery while maintaining high quality.
