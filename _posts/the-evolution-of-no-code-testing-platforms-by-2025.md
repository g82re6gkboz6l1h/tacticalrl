## No-Code Testing Platforms: A Quick Overview
No-code testing platforms simplify QA by enabling users to create automated tests through intuitive drag-and-drop interfaces without technical barriers. These tools foster early QA integration into development, fast iterations, and collaborative workflows within agile environments. From their origins as alternatives to manual QA, they’re now advancing into AI-driven, scalable solutions.

### What Sets No-Code Testing Platforms Apart?
- **Accessibility**: Usable by both technical and non-technical teams.
- **Efficiency**: Reduced dependency on time-consuming coding.
- **Scalability**: Seamlessly adaptable to complex technology stacks.
