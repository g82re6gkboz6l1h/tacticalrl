## Why Traditional QA Methods Are Outdated
Conventional QA processes that rely heavily on coding and scripting are becoming obsolete. They demand specialized skills, causing disconnects between testers and developers and slowing delivery cycles.

### Major Drawbacks of Traditional Testing:
1. **Complex scripting**: Writing and maintaining scripts is time-consuming for Agile environments.
2. **Resource strain**: Specialized testers increase costs, especially for smaller teams.
3. **Higher error rates**: Manual processes risk missing bugs and inconsistencies.
4. **Team silos**: Minimal collaboration between testers and developers impacts project flow.

No-code testing platforms solve these issues. They empower team members without coding expertise to contribute actively, removing QA bottlenecks in Agile workflows.
