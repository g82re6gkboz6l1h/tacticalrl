## Key Challenges Addressed by No-Code Solutions

### 1. **Complexity of Traditional Testing**
Traditional testing relies on intricate code and frameworks, making it inaccessible to non-specialists. As software grows complex, creating effective test cases becomes ever more challenging.

### 2. **Time and Resource-Intensive Testing**
Manual coding in traditional testing consumes significant time and resources, leading to tighter deadlines where testing may be rushed or incomplete.

### 3. **Limited Collaboration**
Non-technical roles like product managers often lack involvement in testing workflows, creating a gap in leveraging user-centric insights.

### 4. **Scalability Barriers**
As agile development cycles accelerate, maintaining accurate test coverage across scaled applications becomes a major hurdle.
