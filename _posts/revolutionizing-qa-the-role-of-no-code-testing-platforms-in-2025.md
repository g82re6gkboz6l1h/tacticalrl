## Key Benefits of No-Code Testing Platforms
- **Empowers Non-Tech Teams:** Business stakeholders and managers can participate in QA without technical expertise.
- **Faster Workflow:** Drag-and-drop interfaces reduce setup time for automated and manual testing.
- **Lower Costs:** Minimized reliance on QA developers reduces overall expenditures.
- **Enhanced Scalability:** Adaptable tools suited for projects of any complexity.
