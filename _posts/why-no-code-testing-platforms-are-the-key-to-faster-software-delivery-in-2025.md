## Traditional Testing: A Roadblock in Software Delivery

Traditional testing methods come with a host of inefficiencies, including:

### **1. Time-Consuming Processes**
Detailed coding for test cases can take weeks or months, delaying launches.

### **2. High Operational Costs**
Hiring specialized QA staff drains resources better spent on innovation.

### **3. Complex Frameworks**
Recurrent updates and maintenance hinder usability.

### **4. Scalability Issues**
Expanding test coverages requires significant manual interventions.

These drawbacks affect time-to-market and complicate quality assurance. Thankfully, no-code platforms offer innovative solutions to overcome these challenges.

Pre-defined workflows and reusable components accelerate test creation and deployment.
