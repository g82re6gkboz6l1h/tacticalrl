## The Shortcomings of Manual Testing

Manual testing has been the default for decades, but modern methodologies such as Agile and DevOps have exposed its limitations:

1. **Time-Intensive**
   Large-scale manual testing can delay delivery timelines, especially with increasing test requirements per sprint or release.

2. **Error-Prone**
   Human fatigue often results in overlooked bugs during repetitive tasks.

3. **Lacks Scalability**
   Expanding manual efforts to meet high-volume testing needs is inefficient and costly.

4. **Limited Test Coverage**
   Deadlines often force testers to skip non-critical scenarios, leaving vulnerabilities.

5. **Cost Overruns**
   Hiring and maintaining large manual QA teams is prohibitively expensive for startups and SMBs.

These challenges underscore the urgency of transitioning to a modern, automated QA process.
