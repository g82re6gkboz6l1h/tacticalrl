### 1. **Inclusive Testing Across Teams**
Break down the traditional silos in QA by enabling non-technical stakeholders—analysts, managers, and product owners—to actively shape testing processes without the need for coding.

No-code platforms automate repetitive tasks, making scalability and faster iterations seamless for QA teams.

### 3. **Cost Efficiency**
Reducing script maintenance and easing test creation lowers QA expenses, delivering better ROI for organizations.

### 4. **Improved Team Collaboration**
Enhanced collaboration across departments ensures tests align directly with quality and customer experience goals.
