## Evolution of No-Code Testing Platforms

No-code platforms started gaining traction in the early 2010s, initially catering to app and website development. By the mid-2020s, they had grown into versatile solutions, democratizing software testing by removing coding barriers. With these tools, team members of diverse technical backgrounds can now actively contribute to quality assurance.

### Key Drivers for Innovation in No-Code Testing:

1. **AI Advancement:** AI is revolutionizing no-code testing, enabling tools like Zof AI to handle complex workflows and predict edge cases. 
2. **User Accessibility:** Intuitive interfaces bridge the gap between technical and non-technical team members.
3. **Modern Software Complexity:** Tools adept at addressing cloud-native architectures, APIs, and microservices emerged to meet evolving demands.

By 2025, no-code testing tools became indispensable in software workflows.
