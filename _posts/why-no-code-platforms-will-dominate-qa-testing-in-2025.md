## Challenges Facing Traditional QA Methods

By 2025, traditional QA methods will fall short due to several challenges:
1. **Demand for Faster Feedback Loops**: The rapid pace of software development requires immediate testing results, which traditional methods struggle to match.
2. **Skill Shortages**: Reliance on programming-heavy testing limits access to skilled professionals.
3. **High Maintenance Costs**: Custom scripts require ongoing maintenance, driving up costs.
4. **Scaling Inefficiencies**: QA teams find it difficult to handle the complexities of modern software development without innovative tools like no-code platforms.
