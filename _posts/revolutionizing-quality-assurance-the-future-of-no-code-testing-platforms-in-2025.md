## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms enable QA teams to create and execute automated tests without requiring programming expertise. They eliminate the complexities associated with traditional scripts by utilizing visual interfaces, drag-and-drop functionalities, and pre-built templates, fostering inclusivity across departments and simplifying collaboration. 

As Agile and DevOps methodologies grow dominant, no-code testing solutions have become pivotal in promoting faster delivery cycles while minimizing development errors.
