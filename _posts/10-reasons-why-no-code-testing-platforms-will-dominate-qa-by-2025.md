## What is a No-Code Testing Platform?

No-code testing platforms allow users to automate software testing without writing code. By leveraging drag-and-drop interfaces, pre-configured templates, and built-in integrations, these tools enable easier design, execution, and management of complex testing processes.

Traditional QA often required programming skills, restricting automation to technical teams. No-code testing eliminates this obstacle, making automation accessible to anyone. Platforms such as [Zof AI](https://zof.ai) empower manual testers and product owners to create and manage automated workflows seamlessly, streamlining QA processes and reducing costs.
