Software testing has historically been complex, requiring coding expertise and manual intervention. Emerging no-code platforms now empower QA teams to test software faster and simpler—reducing resource demands without compromising quality. 1. **Zof AI**  
   Groundbreaking AI-driven testing at [https://zof.ai](https://zof.ai), enabling effortless creation and management of automated tests.
