## Overview of No-Code Platforms and Their Impact on QA

No-code platforms empower users to build, test, and deploy applications without technical expertise. Intuitive interfaces and drag-and-drop workflows are revolutionizing QA, enabling quicker test creation, scalability, and collaborative testing environments. These platforms are integral to Agile and DevOps practices, ensuring efficient workflows and reduced human error.

AI is redefining no-code testing with automation, predictive analytics, and self-maintenance. It offers dynamic optimizations, natural language processing (NLP) for plain language test creation, and computer vision for visual validation. By 2025, AI will elevate these platforms to adapt methodologies, forecast risks, and empower QA teams in real-time.
