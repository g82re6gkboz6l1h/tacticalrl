## What Are No-Code Testing Platforms?

No-code testing platforms empower QA teams and non-technical users to create automated test cases without coding. Leveraging drag-and-drop functionality and pre-built modules, these tools are democratizing software quality assurance. From dynamic tests to accelerated debugging processes, no-code solutions eliminate traditional coding hurdles and foster teamwork across diverse fields.
