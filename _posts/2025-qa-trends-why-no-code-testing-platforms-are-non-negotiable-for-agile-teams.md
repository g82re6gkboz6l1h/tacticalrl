## The Evolution of Agile QA: Why Traditional Testing Falls Behind

Agile fosters faster delivery, but its need for swift iterations and team collaboration exposes traditional testing’s shortcomings:

1. **Manual Testing Slows Sprint Progress**  – Agile’s fast-paced sprints render slow, manual test case creation incompatible.
2. **Coding Barriers Hurt Collaboration**  – Coding-intensive QA tools discourage non-technical team members’ involvement, forming silos.
3. **Struggles with CI/CD Integration**  – Continuous integration/deployment demands immediate automated testing, where traditional methods falter.
