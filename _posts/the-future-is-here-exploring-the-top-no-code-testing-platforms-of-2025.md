## What is No-Code Testing?

No-code testing platforms are designed to simplify software testing by eliminating code-writing barriers. These tools feature drag-and-drop interfaces, AI-driven automation, and intuitive workflows, empowering non-developers to contribute to quality assurance processes seamlessly.

Whether launching a SaaS tool, mobile app, or a web-based platform, no-code testing enables businesses to:

- **Speed up testing cycles:** Accelerating deployment in Agile frameworks.
- **Cut costs:** Reducing reliance on coding experts.
- **Increase accuracy:** Leveraging AI automation to minimize human errors.
