## The Shift to No-Code Testing in Quality Assurance

Traditionally, developers relied on code-based tools like Selenium, requiring technical scripting expertise. No-code testing platforms have disrupted this norm by offering drag-and-drop interfaces, pre-built templates, and codeless workflows. Non-technical users, such as business analysts and product managers, can now contribute directly, bridging teams while expediting the process.
