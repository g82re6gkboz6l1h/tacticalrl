## The Evolution of QA Testing Methods

### From Manual Testing to Scripted Automation
Manual testing dominated QA for many years, requiring human effort to identify errors. However, this was time-consuming and prone to mistakes. Automated testing emerged, introducing predefined scripts that performed tests faster while reducing errors. Tools like Selenium and Appium became popular but required technical scripting knowledge.

### The Revolutionary Shift to No-Code Testing
No-code testing platforms are the natural progression from scripted automation. These tools allow testers to create automated test cases without coding background, making testing accessible to all. No-code technology has democratized QA, saving time and optimizing resources. Innovative companies such as **Zof AI** have integrated artificial intelligence (AI), further enhancing the efficiency of no-code testing.
