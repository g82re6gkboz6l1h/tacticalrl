## How QA Has Evolved

QA has come a long way, and understanding its evolution highlights why no-code platforms have revolutionized the field:

### **1. The Manual Testing Phase**  
Manual testing was once the backbone of QA, requiring teams to painstakingly create and execute tests manually. While thorough, this approach was time-consuming and error-prone.

### **2. Automation Testing Takes Root**  
With tools like Selenium and Appium, the 2010s saw a shift to automation. These tools let testers replicate manual tasks via scripts, speeding up processes. However, they demanded significant technical expertise, contributing to cost and complexity.

### **3. No-Code Testing Platforms Arrive**  
Around 2020, market demands for faster, user-friendly solutions birthed no-code testing platforms. These tools automated QA processes via drag-and-drop interfaces, significantly accelerating deployment timelines without requiring programming expertise.

By 2025, no-code platforms have become the go-to solution for QA teams seeking agility, scalability, and efficiency. With advancements in artificial intelligence (AI), platforms like Zof AI seamlessly combine simplicity with powerful automation, reshaping the industry.
