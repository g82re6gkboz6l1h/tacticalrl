Before no-code solutions, automation required skilled testers proficient in coding. Now, visual drag-and-drop interfaces enable anyone on the QA team, regardless of their technical expertise, to contribute meaningfully. This inclusivity amplifies team capabilities.

### **Faster Testing Processes**
Creating and troubleshooting scripts often consumes time. No-code platforms solve this with pre-built templates and components that QA teams can deploy instantly. This accelerates test creation, execution, and resolution of software bugs.
