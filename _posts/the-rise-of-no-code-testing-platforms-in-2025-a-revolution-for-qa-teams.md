## Understanding No-Code Testing Platforms

No-code testing platforms eliminate the traditional barriers of coding requirements. These user-friendly tools offer drag-and-drop interfaces, pre-configured modules, and automation, making even complex testing procedures accessible to non-technical professionals. 

Previously confined to developers or specialized QA testers, testing activities can now be effortlessly managed by a wider range of users—from product managers to marketing professionals. This democratization of QA breaks down silos, fosters collaboration, and creates opportunities for faster, more comprehensive testing workflows.

### 3. DevOps Alignment
