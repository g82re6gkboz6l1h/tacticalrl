## Key Challenges in Agile Testing

Agile teams encounter frequent obstacles in their testing workflows:

1. **Limited Sprint Time**: Short sprint cycles leave little room for manual testing.
2. **Skill Gaps**: Not all team members possess technical skills for creating and managing test scripts.
3. **Scalability Issues**: Complexity in applications makes manual testing impractical.
4. **Balancing Speed and Quality**: Speed often undermines meticulous testing, risking software stability.
5. **Adaptability to Rapid Changes**: Continuous development requires nimble and adaptive testing tools.
