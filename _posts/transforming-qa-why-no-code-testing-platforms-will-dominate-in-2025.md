## The Evolution of Testing: From Manual to No-Code

### 1. **Manual Testing Era**
Initially, testing was performed manually, requiring testers to click through application interfaces and document results. While effective for smaller-scale software, manual testing was inefficient for large projects, prone to errors, and struggled to keep up with technological growth.

### 2. **Automated Testing Systems**
The late 1990s heralded the era of automated testing. Systems like Selenium allowed QA professionals to create test scripts using languages such as Java or Python. Automation improved testing scalability and accuracy but necessitated coding knowledge, creating skill gaps between QA testers and developers.

### 3. **No-Code Revolution**
Today’s market is embracing no-code testing platforms, bridging the gap between technical and non-technical stakeholders. Leading platforms such as Zof AI leverage artificial intelligence to automate test creation and streamline QA processes—effectively democratizing testing and removing barriers to entry.
