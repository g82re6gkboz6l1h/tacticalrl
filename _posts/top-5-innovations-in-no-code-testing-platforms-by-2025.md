5. What’s Next for No-Code Testing Platforms?

Exciting innovations await no-code testing platforms:

- **Predictive Analytics**: AI-powered insights to forecast testing trends for proactive solutions.
- **Localized Testing**: Simplified testing for global audiences with enhanced internationalization features.
