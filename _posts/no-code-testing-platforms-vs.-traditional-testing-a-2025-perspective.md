### What’s The Difference: No-Code vs. Traditional Testing?

Traditional testing relies heavily on manual processes or code-driven frameworks such as Selenium or JUnit, which require significant technical expertise to operate efficiently. These methods often involve high maintenance and longer test cycles due to their code-heavy nature. This means delays, skill bottlenecks, and added costs.

On the other hand, no-code testing platforms eliminate coding altogether by introducing drag-and-drop interfaces powered by AI. These tools are easy to use, reduce errors, and facilitate collaborative workflows, making testing accessible even to non-technical teams like product managers and marketers.
